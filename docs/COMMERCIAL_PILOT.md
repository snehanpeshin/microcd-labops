# Commercial Pilot Pack

## Ideal first customer

A 3–15 person diagnostics, microfluidics, laboratory-hardware, or translational engineering team using spreadsheets and shared folders for test reports and supplier records, without a budget or need for a full enterprise QMS/PLM rollout.

## Discovery questions

1. Which report or traceability record causes the most rework today?
2. Who creates, reviews, and later searches for that record?
3. Which source files and acceptance criteria must remain connected?
4. What data classifications or customer agreements limit cloud use?
5. What measurable outcome would make a four-week pilot worthwhile?

## Four-week pilot

- Week 1: map one workflow and establish non-sensitive sample records.
- Week 2: configure template, roles, criteria, and supplier/component taxonomy.
- Week 3: run the workflow with a real but approved development record.
- Week 4: compare completion time, missing information, search effort, and reviewer feedback.

## Founder evidence still needed

Customer interviews, willingness-to-pay evidence, support capacity, target retention terms, approved service providers, incident owner, legal entity/billing address, signed terms, and documented product-security review. Do not add logos, testimonials, partnerships, compliance marks, or customer metrics without permission and evidence.
